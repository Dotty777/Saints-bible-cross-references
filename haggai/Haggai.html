Haggai 2:1

In the seventh month, on the twenty-first day of the month, the Lord spoke through Haggai the prophet, saying:

  1. Leviticus 23:34-36 Calendar correspondence: month seven, day twenty-one within the Feast of Tents

    Haggai dates the divine message to the twenty-first day of the seventh month. Leviticus sets the Feast of Tents on the fifteenth day of that same month for seven days, followed by an eighth-day closing observance. Counting the fifteenth as the first festival day places the twenty-first on the seventh day of that prescribed calendar. This is a calendar inference from the stated dates, not a claim that Haggai explicitly reports the audience celebrating the feast or calls its date the eighth day.

    Leviticus 23:36 For seven days you shall bring whole burnt offerings unto the Lord. The eighth day shall be a holy convocation for you, and you shall bring whole burnt offerings unto the Lord. It is a closing observance; you shall do no laborious work.
