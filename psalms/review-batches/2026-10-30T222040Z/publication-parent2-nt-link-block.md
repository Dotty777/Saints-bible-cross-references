## Separate New Testament tracker

The hourly [New Testament tracker](new-testament/README.md) begins with Matthew, in the exact Salvation → Discipleship → Glory order from [canon.json](canon.json). Its counts, review batches and continuation are saved separately in [new-testament/progress.json](new-testament/progress.json). The Old Testament totals and continuation below belong to the existing Old Testament task. The New Testament hourly target is **500 newly completed distinct source verses with cross-references**, counting each source verse once and tracking target-passage pairs separately.
