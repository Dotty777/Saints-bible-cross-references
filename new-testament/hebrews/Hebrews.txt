Hebrews 1:1
In many portions and in many ways, God spoke long ago to the fathers in the prophets,
  2 Peter 1:21 — thematic parallel: Both ascribe prophetic communication to God. Hebrews says God spoke long ago to the fathers in prophets in many portions and ways; Peter says human beings spoke from God, borne along by the Holy Spirit rather than human will. The timing, means and Spirit detail are not treated as identical wording.
    2 Peter 1:21 For prophecy was never borne along by human will, but human beings spoke from God as they were borne along by the Holy Spirit.

Hebrews 1:2
but in these last days He spoke to us in a Son, whom He appointed heir of all things, through whom He also made the ages.
  Colossians 1:16 — thematic parallel: Both describe creative agency through the figure being discussed. Hebrews says God made the ages through the Son whom He appointed heir; Colossians says all things were created in, through and for Him. Ages is not silently replaced with all things, and the distinct in/through/for and heir clauses remain.
    Colossians 1:16 because in Him all things were created, in the heavens and upon the earth, visible and invisible, whether thrones or dominions or rulers or authorities. All things have been created through Him and for Him.

The sustaining descriptions are compared without importing every additional claim into the other unit.
    Colossians 1:17 And He is before all things, and in Him all things hold together.

Hebrews 6:16
For people swear by one greater, and among them an oath for confirmation ends every dispute.
  Exodus 22:9–22:10 — oath confirming a disputed custody matter: Exodus specifies an oath by God between parties about entrusted property, accepted by the owner. This supplies a concrete case of an oath settling a dispute as Hebrews describes. The particular legal rule is not made a complete definition of every human oath.
    Exodus 22:9 And if a man gives his neighbour a beast of burden, or a calf, or a sheep, or any beast to keep, and it is injured, or dies, or is taken captive, and no one knows,
    Exodus 22:10 an oath by God shall be between them that he has not acted dishonestly concerning any of his neighbour’s deposit. The owner shall accept it, and he shall not make restitution.

Hebrews 6:17

  Psalms 109:4 — divine oath and unchanged purpose: The Lord's oath and will-not-change-His-purpose statement in the priestly Psalm give a specific parallel to God's oath showing unchanging purpose. Hebrews here concerns heirs of the Abraham promise; the Psalm concerns priesthood. The two oath occasions are not made identical.
    Psalms 109:4 The Lord swore and will not change His purpose: You are a priest for ever according to the order of Melchizedek.
