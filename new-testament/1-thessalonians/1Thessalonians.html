  1. 1 Thessalonians 3:13 internal prayer parallel

    Both internal prayers look to holiness and blamelessness at Jesus' coming. The earlier concerns hearts blameless in holiness before God the Father at the Lord Jesus' coming with all His saints; the closing prayer names the God of peace and whole spirit, soul and body. This does not independently settle a complete metaphysical account of human components.
