2 Corinthians 13:10
For this reason I write these things while absent, so that when present I may not act sharply according to the authority the Lord gave me, for building up and not for pulling down.
  2 Corinthians 10:8 — internal repeated purpose: Both explicitly say the Lord gave authority for building up and not pulling down. The earlier defense of that authority is here applied to writing while absent to avoid sharp treatment when present.
    2 Corinthians 10:8 For even if I boast somewhat more abundantly concerning our authority, which the Lord gave for building you up and not for pulling you down, I will not be put to shame.

2 Corinthians 13:11
Finally, brothers, rejoice, be restored, be encouraged, be of the same mind, be at peace; and the God of love and peace will be with you.
  Philippians 4:9 — closing exhortation parallel: Both connect instructions for community conduct with the assurance that the God of peace will be with the recipients. Corinthians also names love and urges restoration, shared mind and peace; Philippians calls for practicing what was learned and received.
    Philippians 4:9 Practice the things you learned and received and heard and saw in me; and the God of peace will be with you.

2 Corinthians 13:12
Greet one another with a holy kiss. All the saints greet you.
  1 Corinthians 16:20 — closing greeting parallel: Both combine a holy-kiss greeting instruction with greetings from fellow believers. The source says all the saints and the target all the brothers, with the two greeting clauses in a different order.
    1 Corinthians 16:20 All the brothers greet you. Greet one another with a holy kiss.

2 Corinthians 13:13
The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, and the love of God, and the fellowship of the Holy Spirit be with you all.
  Matthew 28:19 — related threefold formula: Both include the Holy Spirit within a threefold formula. Corinthians names the Lord Jesus Christ, God and the Holy Spirit with grace, love and fellowship; Matthew names Father, Son and Holy Spirit in the baptismal commission. The named titles, actions and formulations are kept distinct.
    Matthew 28:19 Go therefore and make disciples of all the nations, baptizing them into the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,
