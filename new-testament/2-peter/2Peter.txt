2 Peter 1:1
Simeon Peter, a slave and apostle of Jesus Christ, to those who have received a faith of equal honor with ours through the righteousness of our God and Savior Jesus Christ:
  1 Peter 1:1 — Passage-supported comparison: Both openings identify Peter as an apostle of Jesus Christ addressing recipients. This opening additionally calls him Simeon and a slave, and describes equal-honor faith through righteousness; the earlier address names chosen resident aliens of the dispersion and their locations.
    1 Peter 1:1 Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ, to the chosen resident aliens of the dispersion in Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia,

2 Peter 1:2

  1 Peter 1:2 — Passage-supported comparison: Both greetings ask that grace and peace be multiplied to the recipients. The earlier greeting speaks of foreknowledge, sanctification, obedience and sprinkling; this one locates the blessing in knowledge of God and Jesus our Lord.
    1 Peter 1:2 according to the foreknowledge of God the Father, in sanctification by the Spirit, for obedience and sprinkling with the blood of Jesus Christ: May grace and peace be multiplied to you.

    Psalms 89:4 For a thousand years in Thine eyes are like yesterday, which passed, and a watch in the night.

  Romans 2:4 — Passage-supported comparison: Paul says God's kindness leads toward repentance in a context of kindness, restraint and patience.     Romans 2:4 Or do you despise the wealth of His kindness and forbearance and patience, not knowing that God’s kindness leads you to repentance?

2 Peter 3:10

  1 Thessalonians 5:2 — Passage-supported comparison: Paul likewise says the day of the Lord comes like a thief in the night. Peter uses the thief comparison and adds cosmic dissolution and works not being found; those additions are not ascribed to this Thessalonians verse.
    1 Thessalonians 5:2 For you yourselves know exactly that the day of the Lord comes in this way, like a thief in the night.

2 Peter 3:12

    2 Peter 3:10 But the day of the Lord will come like a thief. On that day the heavens will pass away with a rushing roar, and the elements will be dissolved by burning heat, and the earth and the works in it will not be found.
