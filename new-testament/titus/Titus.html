Philippians names the brothers with the speaker, all saints and the Lord Jesus Christ's grace with the recipients' spirit; Titus names all those with me, people who love us in faith and grace with all of you. The household detail and longer divine title in Philippians are not inserted into Titus or used to locate the writing.
